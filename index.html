Our Story

Bringing the Sweet
to Every Celebration

Baby Bird's Creamery is a boutique ice cream cart rental experience designed to make your celebration unforgettable — whether it's an intimate birthday gathering, a dreamy baby shower, an elegant wedding, a vibrant quinceañera, or a polished corporate event.

Based in Southern California, we bring charm, creativity, and sweetness right to your venue. Every detail is handled with care so you can focus on what matters most: celebrating the people you love.

We believe every event deserves one magical, delicious moment your guests talk about for years. That's exactly what we deliver — one scoop at a time.
